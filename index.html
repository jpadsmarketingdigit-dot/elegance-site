FAQ

Your Questions, Answered

Can't find what you need? Text us any time at (508) 493-7469.

How do you calculate my price?

Our online calculator uses your home size, rooms and the type of cleaning (or the carpet rooms, rugs and furniture you add). We confirm the final price after reviewing a few photos or a quick visit.

How long does carpet or upholstery take to dry?

Most carpets and fabrics are dry in about 4 to 8 hours, depending on the material, humidity and air flow. We groom the fibers and help speed up drying before we leave.

Are your products safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We choose products and methods that are safe for families and pets once dry, and we can treat pet odors and accidents at the source.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not necessarily. Many clients give us access instructions. For the first visit we like to do a quick walkthrough with you.

Can you remove every stain?

We remove the large majority of everyday stains. Some old, set-in or dye-based stains can only be lightened; we always tell you what to expect before we start.

Which areas do you serve?

Southeastern Massachusetts, the South Shore, Greater Boston and Rhode Island. Check your town on the map below.

Our process

How we deep clean fabric & fibers

Inspect & test

We check the fiber type, colorfastness and problem spots before anything touches the fabric.

Pre-treat

Targeted solutions loosen stains, spills, pet accidents and ground-in soil.

Deep extraction

Hot-water extraction rinses deep into the fibers and pulls the dirt out.

Groom & dry

We groom the pile, speed up drying and do a final walkthrough with you.
